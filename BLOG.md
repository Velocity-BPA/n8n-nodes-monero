# Automate Monero Transactions with Our New n8n Community Node

We're excited to announce the release of n8n-nodes-monero, a powerful new community node that brings Monero (XMR) cryptocurrency automation directly into your n8n workflows!

## The Challenge

As privacy-focused cryptocurrencies gain traction, businesses and developers need reliable ways to integrate Monero transactions into their automated processes. Whether you're managing payments, monitoring wallet balances, or tracking mining operations, doing this manually is time-consuming and error-prone. Until now, there hasn't been a straightforward way to incorporate Monero operations into n8n's visual automation platform.

## What Can You Do With It?

The n8n-nodes-monero node gives you comprehensive control over Monero operations directly within your workflows:

**Privacy-First Transactions**: Send and receive XMR with Monero's built-in privacy features, perfect for businesses that prioritize financial confidentiality.

**Wallet Management**: Check balances, view transaction history, and manage multiple wallets without leaving your automation workflow.

**Mining Operations**: Monitor and manage mining activities, allowing you to track performance and automate responses to mining events.

**Seamless Integration**: Combine Monero operations with any of n8n's 400+ integrations - trigger notifications in Slack when payments arrive, log transactions to Google Sheets, or sync wallet data with your CRM.

## Real-World Use Cases

Imagine automatically processing customer refunds in XMR, sending wallet balance alerts to your team when thresholds are reached, or creating comprehensive financial reports that include your Monero transactions alongside traditional payment data. The possibilities are endless when you combine Monero's privacy features with n8n's automation capabilities.

## Getting Started

Installing the node is simple. In your n8n instance, run:


npm install n8n-nodes-monero


Then restart n8n, and you'll find the Monero node available in your node palette. Check out the full documentation and source code on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/Velocity-BPA/n8n-nodes-monero
